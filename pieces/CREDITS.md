# Piece set credits & licenses

The traditional 2D piece sets bundled in Chess Cadet come from the
[lichess](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila) project. All are free for general
use (including commercial) provided their license terms — attribution, and for
GPL keeping the assets under the same license — are honored.

| Set (in app) | Folder | Artist | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classic | `cburnett` | Colin M.L. Burnett | GPLv2+ |
| Merida | `merida` | Armando Hernandez Marroquin | GPLv2+ |
| Chessnut | `chessnut` | Alexis Luengas | Apache 2.0 |

Sources:
- cburnett / merida — https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece
- chessnut — https://github.com/LexLuengas/chessnut-pieces

Other freely-usable sets available from lichess if we want more variety later:
permissive (commercial OK): `chessnut` (Apache), `fantasy`/`spatial`/`celtic`
(MIT, Maurizio Monge), `rhosgfx` (CC0 / public domain), `kiwen-suwi` & `Firi`
(CC BY 4.0). Non-commercial only (CC BY-NC-SA): `maestro`, `staunty`,
`cardinal`, `california`, `gioco`, `tatiana`, and many more.
